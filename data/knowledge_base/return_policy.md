# Return and Exchange Policy - DemoShop India

- Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery.
- Items must be unused, unwashed, and in original packaging with all tags attached.
- Non-returnable items: innerwear, personalized items, gift cards, and items marked "Final Sale".
- If you receive a damaged or wrong item, report it within 48 hours of delivery with clear photos. Our support team will arrange a free pickup.
- Size exchanges are allowed within 7 days of delivery, subject to stock availability.
- To start a return, contact the support team with your order number.
