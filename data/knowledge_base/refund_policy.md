# Refund Policy - DemoShop India

- Refunds are processed only after the returned item reaches our warehouse and passes inspection. Inspection takes 2-3 business days.
- Prepaid orders are refunded to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after the refund is approved.
- COD orders are refunded by bank transfer. Bank details must be submitted only through the secure link sent by our support team. Never share bank details over a phone call.
- The shipping fee is not refunded, except when the item was damaged or the wrong item was sent.
- Refunds are approved by a human support agent. Automated systems and assistants cannot approve refunds.
