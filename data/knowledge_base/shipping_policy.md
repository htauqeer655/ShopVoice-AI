# Shipping Policy - DemoShop India

- Orders are dispatched within 24 hours of order confirmation, excluding Sundays and public holidays.
- Standard delivery takes 4-7 business days to metro cities and 7-10 business days to all other locations in India.
- Express delivery takes 2-3 business days to metro cities and costs Rs. 99.
- Standard shipping is free on orders above Rs. 999. Orders of Rs. 999 or less pay a Rs. 49 shipping fee.
- A tracking link is sent by SMS and email after the order is dispatched.
- Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for orders up to Rs. 5,000 with a Rs. 40 COD fee.
- DemoShop India ships only within India. We do not ship internationally.
